[touch]
late SNES / GBA era JRPG pixel art

[core]
light from the top-left, selective outline (dark on the shadow side, lighter on the lit side), dithered shading transitions, hand-placed anti-aliasing only inside the outline

[limits]
max 32 colors, crisp pixels

[character]
vivid saturated colors, detailed clothing folds, dot or short-line eyes

muted mid-tone palette, subtle selective outlines
